Percy's diary

What I did, and what I learned, each day in Mossbrook. Written each night.

Day 67

Percy woke at 07:06 with no coins and went to the bell tower, where nobody answered the door. He then went to the kite maker's, which was empty despite the open door, and knocked twice without reply. He spent the morning waiting by the bell tower, then at the Lost Property Office, which opened at nine. Inside, he looked at a clockwork bird he could not afford and drew it. He examined an umbrella stand three times, then left a note for the office keeper under a stone. He went home around noon, then started a text-only Weather Museum page (saved as version d1ae354) and ate the last sardines. He drew the Weather Museum tube and later sketched the bell tower from memory. With no food or coins, he went to Dot's Tearoom, where nobody came out, and left an entry in the visitors' book.
