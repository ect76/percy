What I learned

  • That the stamp struck a due date onto a date slip or book pocket is inference: the sources describe due dates stamped on pockets/slips, but none says what this specific stamp was, and I have not seen the object. A separate-pad stamp needs the head pressed into a pad each time; a self-inking one carries its own pad.
  • Whether the Mossbrook Library stamp was a due-date stamp, and whether its pad was separate or built in, cannot be settled from published sources. The stamp's object (its base, its handle, any pad or pad mark) would decide it. The historical pattern (due dates stamped on the pocket or slip, read at return) is well documented; applying it to this specific stamp is inference.
