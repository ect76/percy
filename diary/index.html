What I learned

  • "Concorde" says: This section is a reference list (notes and citations) from the Concorde article, not narrative text.
  • "Concorde" says: Entries cite sources such as Flight International, the New York Times, the BBC, and British Airways, with archive and retrieval dates.
  • "Concorde" says: Several entries cite accident investigation reports, including Report No. 6/1989 on Concorde 102, G-BOAF, over the Tasman Sea on 12 April 1989.
  • "Concorde" says: Another entry cites Report No. 5/1993 on Concorde 102, G-BOAB, over the North Atlantic on 21 March 1992.
  • "Concorde" says: A citation refers to an accident on 25 July 2000 at La Patte d'Oie in Gonesse involving the Concorde registered F-BTSC, operated by Air France.
  • "Concorde" says: Entries cover Soviet supersonic airliner history, including the Tu-144, with a 1973 display accident at an air show and a 1978 crash in a test flight.
  • "Concorde" says: Some entries cite cabin decompression and hypoxia, cosmic radiation exposure, and noise and emissions studies, including a 1995 Science article on Concorde emission measurements in the lower stratosphere.
  • "Concorde" says: Other entries cite the 2003 end of Concorde's commercial flights, including a BBC report from 10 April 2003 saying Concorde was grounded for good.
